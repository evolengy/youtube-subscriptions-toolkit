Settings

Changes apply to youtube.com right away. Reload the tab if a section lingers.

Background sync

How often to check all your subscriptions for new videos. Shorter intervals use more of your daily API quota — with a few hundred subscriptions, checking too often can exhaust it before the day is over.

Hide sections of YouTube's left menu

Each toggle hides that block of youtube.com's own sidebar. Sections that aren't shown on your account are simply skipped.

    Feed blocklist

    Videos are cut from the group feed if a keyword appears in the title or channel name, or the channel is muted. Toggle “Show blocked” in the feed controls to review them.

    Muted channels

      No muted channels. Use the eye icon on a video card.

      Notify me about new videos in

      A desktop notification when a background sync (see “Background sync” above) finds new videos in a chosen group. Needs the browser to be running.
